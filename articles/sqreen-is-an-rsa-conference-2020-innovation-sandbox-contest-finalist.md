I’m excited to share a brief bit of news: Sqreen has been named one of the 10 finalists for the[RSA Conference 2020 Innovation Sandbox Contest](https://web.archive.org/web/20230604010124/https://www.rsaconference.com/usa/the-experience/innovation-programs/innovation-sandbox)!
On Monday, February 24th, I’ll be at the Moscone Center in San Francisco, sharing Sqreen’s unique approach to application security in front of a panel of judges and a live audience of 1,000+ people. The Innovation Sandbox, now in its fifteenth year, has showcased some of our industry’s most inspiring companies, and we’re honored to be added to this list.
I’m so thankful to our 800+ customers who have helped us get to this point, and of course, huge thanks as well to the Sqreen team. There’s a lot more to do as we democratize security and build out the Sqreen platform, and I can’t imagine a better team to do it with.
Are you going to the RSA Conference? If so,[let me know](https://web.archive.org/web/20230604010124/mailto:hey@sqreen.io)— I would love to see you there!
